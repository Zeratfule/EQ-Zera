What it does

A DPS meter, overlays, quest and loot tracking for EverQuest Legends

EQ Zera watches the log file EverQuest Legends writes and turns every line into something you can act on while you play. Per character, per fight, per zone.

Live DPS meter

Per-fight and per-zone damage and healing numbers with fight history. Click a fight to see every hit, miss and resist that made the number, drill into each attack and spell, and scrub a timeline of the pull. Charm pets are counted correctly.

Floating overlays

Small always-on-top meters you leave over the game: damage or healing, scoped to the current fight or the whole zone, plus a farm meter with kills, XP, drops and coin per hour. Lock one and it becomes click-through.

Quest tracker and quest item pop-ups

Track any EverQuest Legends quest from its page and follow its steps as a checklist. Looting an item lights its step, hailing the NPC ticks the hail, and a turn-in completes it with a card and your alert. Loot something a quest needs and a pop-up names the quest and where it starts.

Plane of Sky tracker

Every class's Plane of Sky Test quests with have / need chips per item, item stats on hover, and sorting by closest to done.

Loot, upgrades and where to farm

A running history of what you looted and what each item is for. Zone Loot shows every mob the wiki lists in a zone and what it drops, with an upgrade chip when a drop beats what you wear, and the Build tab lists where to farm your upgrades, zone by zone.

Crafting and wish lists

What you can make from what you hold, what you are one ingredient away from, and every recipe with what you are missing. Wish-list an item and get an alert when it drops or when you enter a zone where it does.

Your character, in 3D

A model of your character with race, sex and face controls for the classic races and Iksar. Preview any wearable item on it from the Gear tab or an item page, one click back to your real gear.

Alerts, timers and recaps

Sound alerts for a charm break, a fading buff, a raid target dying, or any log line you write a rule for. Buff and debuff timers, XP and AA progress, raid-target history, and a death recap that says who killed you and what hit you in the last fifteen seconds.

Install

Install EQ Zera on Windows in a minute

A one-click, per-user install like Discord. No admin prompt, no wizard. It adds a Start menu and desktop shortcut.

Download the installer

eq-zera-Setup.exe is always the newest build. Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

Click through SmartScreen

Windows shows “Windows protected your PC” because the installer is not yet code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway. You see it once.

Turn on logging in game

In EverQuest Legends type /log on. The app finds your log folder on its own, usually under Daybreak Game Company, Installed Games, EverQuest Legends, Logs.

Play

Fight something and the meter fills in. Overlays sit over the game in windowed or borderless mode; exclusive fullscreen cannot be overlaid by anything.

Coming from EQ Legends Companion?

EQ Zera continues where the EverQuest Companion app stopped

The same app, still maintained

EQ Legends Companion (the everquest-companion project by Josh Moyers) ended development in September 2026. EQ Zera is its continuation: the same DPS meter, overlays, Plane of Sky tracker, loot history and alerts, with new releases, a self-updater that works, and an open issue tracker.

Everything added since the fork is listed under What's new: the quest tracker, quest item pop-ups, Zone Loot, upgrade finder, crafting, wish lists, the farm meter, death recap and the 3D character model.

Switching takes one install

EQ Zera reads the same settings folder, so your alerts, sound packs, overlay positions and character history carry over on first launch. Nothing is moved or deleted; the old install keeps working if you leave it.

Looking for the EQ Legends Companion download? The original project no longer publishes builds. The EQ Zera installer is the current one.

Updates and trust

It updates itself, and it tells you the terms

How updates work

An installed copy checks GitHub shortly after launch and every few hours after that. When a newer build is out you get a card on the celebration overlay and a note in Preferences. Nothing downloads until you click, and a downloaded build is applied the next time you close the app.

Every download is verified against the SHA-512 in the release feed, so a corrupted or substituted file fails to install.

What is and is not verified

The installer is unsigned: there is no code-signing certificate for the project yet, which is why SmartScreen warns on first run. Integrity is checked, authorship is not, so publish access to the GitHub repository is the trust root. The full statement is in the repository's SECURITY.md.

EQ Zera carries the upstream license: free to use, modify and share for non-commercial purposes, converting to MIT on the license's schedule.

FAQ

Questions people ask before installing

Is a log parser allowed in EverQuest Legends?

EQ Zera reads the plain-text log file the game writes when you type /log on. It does not read game memory, inject anything, send input, or automate play. Log parsers of this kind have been part of EverQuest for over twenty years.

Is EQ Zera free?

Yes. It is open source under the Functional Source License and there is nothing to buy, no account and no sign-in.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

The installer is Windows 10 and 11 only. Players have run it under Wine and CrossOver; the engine now learns your time zone so timers are right there too, but it is not a supported platform.

Why does Windows warn when I run the installer?

The build is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC”. Click More info, then Run anyway. The download itself is verified against a SHA-512 hash before it installs.

Do the overlays work in fullscreen?

In windowed and borderless windowed mode, yes. Exclusive fullscreen cannot be overlaid by any program, so switch to borderless if you want meters over the game.

Where do the item, mob and quest data come from?

From the EQ Legends wiki, fetched on demand and cached. Quest steps for the tracker are built from the wiki's walkthrough pages.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. No telemetry endpoint is compiled in. Feedback goes out only when you choose to send it, through your own mail app or a GitHub issue, and you see the report first.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Open a GitHub issue, or use Send feedback inside the app, which fills the report in for you.
